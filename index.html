NanoDrop

Dual nano-lancet upper-arm blood collection device for over-the-counter use. It was the first OTC blood-lancing device labeled for the upper arm cleared in the United States.

  • Human-factors, pain/preferred-method, penetration-depth, vacuum, sharps-safety, biocompatibility, sterilization and shelf-life programs
  • CE Mark certification in 2023
